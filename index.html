ONS2 Overview

Built on the Odin1 (Odin's Eye) spatial memory module, ONS2 is an open-source, pluggable navigation stack for industrial inspection—covering mapping, localization, planning, and execution, with live 3DGS monitoring.

What's New in ONS2

Upgrades that take the Odin Navigation Stack beyond ONS1.

01Dramatically faster planning

Plans substantially faster even on low-compute platforms, with smoother execution and nimbler obstacle avoidance.

02Upgraded VLM, bring your own model

Connect a more capable vision-language model to your own workstation for richer scene understanding.

03Built for industrial inspection

Extensive optimizations tuned to real industrial inspection scenarios and long patrol routes.

04End-to-end, fully open source

A complete inspection workflow — simulation to real robots, algorithms to the monitoring platform. See the Real2Sim tutorial.

05Plugin architecture

The stack is built around a plugin-based design, making it straightforward to integrate custom planners.

More on the way

Further capabilities will land in upcoming releases.

Odin1 On-Robot

Working with Odin1

This project showcases multiple demo pipelines built on Odin1. Each demo can be used independently or combined as an integrated stack.

01SLAM

Build 3D maps for fast, persistent relocalization, providing positioning anchors for navigation.

It may take some time to load the 3D model.

02Navigation

Long-range navigation through complex skybridges connecting multiple buildings: one complete round trip of about 640 m, with ONS 2 maintaining precise localization and stable online navigation, free of localization drift or map misalignment.

03Real2Sim Navigation

Replicate real-world sites in Isaac Sim from Q9000 or Odin1 scans, import robot models (e.g., URDF), and run navigation and manipulation tasks.

04Navigation in 3DGS Reconstructed Scenes

Built from Q9000 scanned 3DGS maps, the ONS 2 dashboard also runs in photorealistic reconstructed scenes: here a Go2 quadruped descends an outdoor staircase in simulation, with the viewpoint free to move.


Workflow Overview

Q9000 (.lx) or Odin1 (.olx) captures the site; MindCloud exports three branches—navigation maps, a live 3DGS dashboard, and Isaac Sim scenes—for ONS2 navigation, monitoring, and validation.

System Architecture

Inside ONS2, four layers stay decoupled: a unified robot profile, an ego-centric perception backbone, a three-slot planning stack, and a chassis bridge with inspection tooling—all on a symmetric ROS 1 / ROS 2 runtime.
